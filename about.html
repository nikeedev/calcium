Calcium: Element 20

Attributes: "范皓程" at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Calcium_(Element_-_20)_2.jpg

Specifications:

  • Element name: Ca
  • Atomic number: 20
  • Properties: One of the alkaline metals. Very reactive on contact with moisture.
  • Chemical reactions:
    Reactant(s) Reation way Product(s)
    2Ca (s) + 2H2O (l) -> 2CaOH (aq) + H2 (g)
    Ca2+ (aq) + CO32- (aq) <-> CaCO3 (s)
  • Especially the first reaction has very low entalphy (ΔH = -414 kJ/mol) which means it is very exothermic, aka. it produces a lot of warmth, which is what later causes the hydrogen gas to get lit and thereon creates an explosion, but mainly the reaction between water and calcium causes the explosion, and not the reaction between heat and hydrogen gas (although it does happen on par to strengthen the reaction).
